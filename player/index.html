    Lesson as data: edit and run your own

    A lesson is {title, steps:[{title, say, do:[…]}]} on a 400×500 board. Each step speaks its say line while its do list is drawn in order. Colors: ink blue orange green red yellow brown purple teal grey soft paper or any hex.

    {op:"line", x1,y1,x2,y2}
    {op:"poly", pts:[[x,y],…], close:true}
    {op:"rect", x,y,w,h} · {op:"circle", cx,cy,r}
    {op:"path", d:"M…"} any SVG path
    {op:"text", t, x,y, size, anchor, font:"display|mono|hand"}
    {op:"fill", of:"rect|poly|circle", …same keys, color, a:0.55} scribble-colors a shape
    {op:"cam", x,y,s} pan and zoom to a point
    {op:"clear"} wipe the page · {op:"hold", s:1} pause

    Every drawing op also takes color, lw (line weight) and dur (seconds). A step with pause:true stops at its end until the student presses Continue.
